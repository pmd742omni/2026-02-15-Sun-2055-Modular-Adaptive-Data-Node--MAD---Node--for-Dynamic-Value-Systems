## Version 1.10.0 Ukuhlola
**Ukuhlola** is a Ndebele word that means "testing", "examination", or "inspection". It denotes the process of checking the quality, state, or performance of something. By laying out the rigorous test cases, data collection procedures, and simulated power-interruption trials, *Ukuhlola* establishes the scientific metrics used to prove the prototype's real-world viability, offline survivability, and data security in rural Bulawayo and Tsholotsho.
