## Version 1.0.3 Umklamo
**Umklamo** is a Ndebele word that means "Design" or "Blueprint". Imagine you want to build a super cool spaceship out of cardboard boxes. Before you start cutting and taping, you draw a picture of what it should look like and how the doors will open. "Umklamo" is that special drawing! It's our plan that shows everyone exactly how we are going to build and test our big idea.
