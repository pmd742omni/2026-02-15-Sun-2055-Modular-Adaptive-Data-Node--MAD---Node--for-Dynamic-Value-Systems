## Version 1.9.0 Inqubo
**Inqubo** is a Ndebele word that means "procedure", "process", or "orderly method". It denotes a systematic series of actions taken to achieve an end. By delineating the exact steps required to set up, program, and print the MADN hardware and software stacks, *Inqubo* establishes a transparent engineering methodology, enabling local makers and technicians to construct, run, and maintain data nodes using clear guidelines.
