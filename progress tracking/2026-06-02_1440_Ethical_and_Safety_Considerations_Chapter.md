## Version 1.11.0 Ukuphepha
**Ukuphepha** is a Ndebele word that means "safety", "security", or "protection". It describes the state of being safe and protected from danger. By detailing how the MADN prototype prevents battery fires, keeps electrical components dry and cool, respects community privacy by not using cameras, and guards transaction records locally, *Ukuphepha* ensures that the system is safe to use and builds trust with local farmers and shopkeepers.
