## Version 1.1.1 Umfanekiso
**Umfanekiso** is a Ndebele word that means "picture", "image", or "metaphor". When you are trying to explain a big, complicated idea, sometimes it is easiest to draw a picture or tell a story about something familiar. For example, comparing a complex computer network to a Marula tree! *Umfanekiso* is that helper picture or story that makes the hard stuff easy to see in your mind.
