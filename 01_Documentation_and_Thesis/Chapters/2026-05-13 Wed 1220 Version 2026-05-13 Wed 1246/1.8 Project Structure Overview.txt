​1.8 Project Structure Overview
​This document is systematically organized into six primary chapters, followed by references and supplementary appendices, to provide a comprehensive and structured overview of the MADN project from conception to evaluation:
​Chapter 1: Introduction: Sets the context of the study, defining the background, problem statement, overarching aim, specific objectives, research questions, scope, and the study's overall significance.
​Chapter 2: Literature Review: Explores the theoretical background and state-of-the-art technologies related to edge computing, distributed networks, precision agriculture, embedded computer vision, and resilient commerce. This chapter critically reviews existing systems to explicitly identify the technological gaps the MADN framework aims to fill.
​Chapter 3: Methodology: Outlines the core research design and the methodological approach used to construct the MADN. It details the system requirements (both hardware and software), initial system design (including block and flow diagrams), tools utilized, development procedures, testing methodologies, and essential ethical and safety considerations.
​Chapter 4: System Design and Prototype Development: Provides an in-depth, technical exploration of the MADN architecture. It details the prototype construction combining the Raspberry Pi 4 and Pico W nodes, component integration, programming logic for the four core pillars, as well as a transparent review of development challenges encountered and their respective solutions.
​Chapter 5: Results, Testing and Analysis: Presents the empirical data gathered from rigorous system testing. It covers testing procedures, test results, and data analysis to evaluate system performance. Furthermore, it compares the MADN against existing cloud-reliant systems and offers a comprehensive discussion of the findings in relation to the initial research questions.
​Chapter 6: Conclusions and Recommendations: Summarizes the project's key findings, evaluating its success against the established objectives. This final chapter also outlines the limitations of the current prototype and provides actionable recommendations for future research, system scaling, and real-world deployment.
​References & Appendices: The main document is supported by a comprehensive bibliography formatted in IEEE style. The Appendices provide critical supplementary material, including detailed circuit diagrams (Appendix A), core source code (Appendix B), any relevant questionnaires (Appendix C), a complete Cost Analysis/Bill of Quantities (Appendix D), the project Gantt Chart (Appendix E), and a functional User Manual for the MADN system (Appendix F).
