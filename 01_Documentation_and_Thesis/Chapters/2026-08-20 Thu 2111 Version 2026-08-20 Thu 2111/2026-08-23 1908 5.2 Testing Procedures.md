# 5.2 Testing Procedures

## 5.2.1 Automated Test Harness Architecture
The verification of the Modular Adaptive Data Node (MADN) system is executed using a modular, repeatable `pytest` automation harness. The testing harness validates all layers of the stack across four specialized test modules encompassing 21 discrete test cases:

```
Applications/Web App/backend/
├── test_customer_banking.py       (6 Tests: Wallets, Ledgers, P2P, Voucher Conversion, POS Wallet Debits, Receipt Vault)
├── test_business_operators.py     (4 Tests: Hierarchical RBAC, Operator Access Delegation, Subsystem Permissions)
├── test_multibiz_and_vouchers.py  (5 Tests: Multi-Business Isolation, HMAC Vouchers, Double-Spend Prevention, Receipts)
└── test_stage1_core.py            (6 Tests: Cost Floors, Decay Math, Tender Split, Visitor Gatekeeper, Social Hub, Mesh)
```

## 5.2.2 Test Procedure Breakdown

### Suite 1: Customer Digital Banking & Receipt Vault (`test_customer_banking.py`)
1. **`test_customer_wallet_creation_and_balances`**: Validates wallet account auto-provisioning with format `ACC-2026-XXXXXX` and default zero balances across USD, ZAR, and ZWG.
2. **`test_wallet_topup_and_ledger_hmac`**: Simulates cash deposits across USD and ZAR denominations, validating balance updates and HMAC-SHA256 signature chain creation in `wallet_ledger`.
3. **`test_wallet_p2p_transfer`**: Tests atomic debiting of sender wallet and crediting of recipient wallet under `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` exclusive write lock.
4. **`test_voucher_to_wallet_deposit`**: Tests the conversion of an offline cryptographic QR voucher into spendable liquid balance in the customer's wallet.
5. **`test_pos_checkout_via_wallet_payment`**: Validates direct debit of customer wallet balance during touch POS checkout without fiat cash exchange.
6. **`test_customer_receipt_vault_storage_and_lookup`**: Tests permanent digital receipt archiving into `customer_receipts` table with item enrichment and SHA-256 integrity hash.

### Suite 2: Multi-Tenant RBAC & Operator Access Delegation (`test_business_operators.py`)
1. **`test_assign_and_retrieve_business_operators`**: Verifies dynamic staff role assignments across separate business entities.
2. **`test_granular_permission_evaluation`**: Tests permission checks against specific subsystems (`pos`, `inventory`, `agriculture`, `security`, `social`, `reports`).
3. **`test_operator_revocation`**: Validates immediate deactivation of revoked operators.
4. **`test_business_admin_fallback`**: Ensures system superadmins and business owners maintain override administrative authority.

### Suite 3: Multi-Business Tenancy & Offline Bearer Vouchers (`test_multibiz_and_vouchers.py`)
1. **`test_multi_business_creation_and_isolation`**: Verifies strict database isolation across multi-business stores.
2. **`test_offline_voucher_minting_and_hmac_integrity`**: Tests cryptographic voucher issuance and tamper detection.
3. **`test_voucher_redemption_and_double_spend_prevention`**: Verifies single-use redemption enforcement.
4. **`test_pos_checkout_with_voucher_change_minting`**: Validates automatic offline QR voucher change generation.
5. **`test_structured_receipt_generation`**: Verifies receipt metadata formatting for thermal/PDF printing.

### Suite 4: Stage 1 Core Integration (`test_stage1_core.py`)
1. **`test_agricultural_cost_floor_and_base_price`**: Validates production cost floors \(P_{\text{cost}}\) and base pricing \(P_{\text{base}}\).
2. **`test_continuous_decay_pricing_math`**: Validates continuous exponential decay pricing \(P(t) = P_{\text{cost}} + (P_{\text{base}} - P_{\text{cost}}) \cdot e^{-\lambda t}\).
3. **`test_mixed_tender_split_change_calculation`**: Tests tri-currency tender reconciliation.
4. **`test_visitor_gatekeeper_lifecycle`**: Validates visitor badge issuance and departure checkout.
5. **`test_social_hub_four_paradigms`**: Tests Threads, Carousels, Stories, and Reels with micropayment tipping.
6. **`test_udp_discovery_and_mesh_telemetry`**: Validates UDP multicast beacon processing and LWW telemetry updates.
