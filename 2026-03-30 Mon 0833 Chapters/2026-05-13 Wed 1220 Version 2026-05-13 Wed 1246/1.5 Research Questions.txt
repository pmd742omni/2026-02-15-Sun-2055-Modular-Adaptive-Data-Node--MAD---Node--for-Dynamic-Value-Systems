1.5 Research Questions

To guide the development and evaluation of the MADN framework, this study seeks to answer the following research questions:

​1. How can a Dynamic Resource Scheduler on a Raspberry Pi 4 efficiently aggregate and distribute complex computational tasks across a decentralized network of heterogeneous, idle personal devices in a resource-constrained environment?

​2. To what extent can localized edge processing via TensorFlow Lite, using microclimate data (moisture, pH, temperature) collected from Raspberry Pi Pico W nodes, improve the accuracy of agricultural yield predictions for farmers in Bulawayo and Tsholotsho compared to traditional methods?

​3. How effectively can a localized security alert system utilize edge-triggered sensors (PIR, ultrasonic) to perform real-time intrusion detection and perimeter security alerts without relying on continuous cloud connectivity?

​4. What architectural design is required to establish a resilient, local-first Distributed Micro-Transaction Ledger that ensures seamless commercial operations and stock synchronization during periods of intermittent internet access?
