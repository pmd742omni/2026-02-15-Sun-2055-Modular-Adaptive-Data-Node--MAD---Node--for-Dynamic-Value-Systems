1.4 Objectives of the Project
​
To achieve the overarching aim, the project will be guided by the following specific objectives:

​1. To develop the MADN Orchestrator: To design and deploy a core infrastructure on a Raspberry Pi 4 Model B featuring a Dynamic Resource Scheduler capable of identifying, aggregating, and distributing computational tasks across idle guest devices (smartphones, laptops) and to establish an Open-Source API Gateway for system extensibility.

​2. To implement the Agri-Analytics Engine: To configure Raspberry Pi Pico W edge nodes for real-time environmental data collection (moisture, pH, temperature) and process this data through a localized TensorFlow Lite yield prediction model, tailored to the microclimates of Bulawayo and Tsholotsho.

3. ​To engineer the Edge-Vision Gateway: To integrate low-power edge sensors (PIR and ultrasonic tripwires) with an Intelligent Surveillance Hub on the primary node, utilizing OpenCV for real-time motion detection, object recognition, and localized perimeter security.

​4. To deploy the Mesh-POS System: To construct a local-first, resilient Distributed Micro-Transaction Ledger and an Inventory Synchronization Mesh that enables uninterrupted commercial transactions and digital stock monitoring for local vendors during connectivity fluctuations.
