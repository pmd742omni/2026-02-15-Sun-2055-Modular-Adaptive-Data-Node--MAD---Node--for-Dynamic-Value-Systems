# Side Quest 001: Intuitive Understanding of the Research Design

## Objective
The primary goal of this side quest is to develop a deep, intuitive understanding of the Design Science Research (DSR) paradigm as applied to the MADN project. While the main research documentation focus on academic rigor, this quest allows for creative metaphors, mental models, and unconventional explorations to internalize *why* we are using this specific design.

## Narrative
If the MADN is a tree, the Research Design is the soil and the roots. This quest helps us understand how the roots grip the earth and how the soil provides the nutrients needed for the tree to grow tall and strong in the harsh environments of Bulawayo and Tsholotsho.

## Activities
- Creating metaphors for the DSR process.
- Visualizing the flow of data as a "dynamic value system".
- Exploring the "what if" scenarios of decentralized compute.
