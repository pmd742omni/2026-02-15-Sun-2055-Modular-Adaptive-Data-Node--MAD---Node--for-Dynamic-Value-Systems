🏗️ Track 1: Tri-Node Decentralized Architecture

The Modular Adaptive Data Node (MADN) operates as an autonomous, self-healing tri-node cluster engineered for zero-internet environments and sovereign local edge execution.

1. Operator Node (Web UI :8000)

Zero-installation web client executing inside modern browsers. Renders real-time telemetry, touch-friendly POS registers, social feeds, and digital customer wallets.

2. Standalone Storage Hub (:8002)

Autonomous storage hub. Automatically broadcasts on local Wi-Fi to keep records synchronized across devices without internet.

3. Vault Coordinator (:8000)

Security coordinator. Ensures zero-trust credential encryption, instant database updates, and tamper-proof digital receipts.

🏢 Track 2: Genesis Bootstrap Account & Sovereign Operator Hierarchy

MADN utilizes a sovereign ownership lifecycle: clean node deployments start with a temporary genesis bootstrap credential that transforms into your permanent Sovereign Administrator upon first customization.

1. Genesis Bootstrap Default

When MADN is provisioned on a new system or clean vault, it initializes with a temporary bootstrap account (admin / Password123!) to allow immediate offline operator login.

INITIAL SETUP ONLY

2. Sovereign Admin Transformation

As soon as you customize your profile (setting your full name, personal username, password, or avatar), the bootstrap account morphs into the node's permanent Sovereign Administrator.

PERMANENT OWNER

3. Operator Privilege Delegation

All other accounts in the system are Operators (Agronomists, Guards, Merchants, Customers, Guests). Privileges and store access are granted, managed, and revoked exclusively by the Sovereign Admin.

SCOPED PERMISSIONS

Enterprise Multi-Store RBAC:

  1. Live Store Switcher: Switch active context via the STORE dropdown in the top header without logging out.
  2. Store Operator Scoping: Assign staff to specific stores (e.g. Green Valley Farm vs. Sunrise Dairy) with distinct permissions.
  3. Zero Default Privilege Bleed: Subsystems (agriculture, pos, security, banking) are evaluated strictly against active operator grants.

🌾 Track 3: Fresh Produce Lifecycle & Smart Freshness Pricing

Fresh crops lose value as they sit on the shelf. The system automatically offers gradual discounts as produce nears its sell-by date to sell stock quickly and prevent food waste, while strictly ensuring the selling price never drops below what it cost the farmer to grow.

How Smart Freshness Pricing Works:

🌱 1. Fresh Harvest

Opens at standard retail price when freshly harvested.

⏳ 2. Automatic Discount

Gradually discounts over time to speed up inventory turnover.

🛡️ 3. Guaranteed Floor

Prices never drop below direct input costs, protecting farmer livelihood.

🪙 Track 4: Multi-Currency POS, Mixed Tender & Bearer Vouchers

In multi-currency economies (USD, ZAR, ZWG), physical small change is often unavailable. MADN allows customers to tender mixed currencies and mints cryptographic offline bearer vouchers for remaining change.

Split Mixed Tender

Pay with any combination of USD cash, ZAR notes, and digital wallet balance in a single checkout transaction.

Offline QR Bearer Vouchers

Unpaid change is minted as an HMAC-SHA256 signed QR voucher redeemable at any participating node or deposit to wallet.
